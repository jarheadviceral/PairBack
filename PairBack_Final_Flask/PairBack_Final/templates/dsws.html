Report or claim an item

  1. Check where you last had the item and ask the area's security guard.
  2. Visit MVP 101, submit the Missing Item Form, or email the item description, date, and approximate time lost.
  3. Bring a valid ID or proof of ownership when claiming. Staff must verify ownership before release.

Non-valuable items → DSWS

Books, bags, clothing, water jugs, notebooks, school supplies, umbrellas, costumes and props, hand fans, and sports equipment.

Valuable items → Xavier Hall

Money, IDs, electronics, glasses, flash drives, keys, and musical instruments are generally referred to OADSA at Xavier Hall, according to the team's supplied guidance. Confirm the current receiving office with DSWS.
